How to read it

Points scored and allowed per 100 possessions, so pace is taken out. Top right is good at both ends; bottom left is struggling at both. The diagonal through the middle is where offence and defence cancel out.

What Drives ItFour Factors
Shooting, turnovers, rebounding and free throws — roughly in that order of importance. Each is paired with the opponent's version, because conceding a 60% eFG is as much a fact about your defence as your own shooting is about your offence.
