📐 What Drives It Four Factors
Shooting, rebounding, getting to the line and taking care of the ball — at both ends. Every factor is scored against the rest of this tournament and signed so that positive is good, then the eight are added up. That total is the projection; the last columns check it against where each team finished.
Extra possessions vs. shooting
Across: offensive rebounds plus turnovers forced, less the same given up, per game — how many more shots a team gets than its opponent. Up: how well it makes them. The dashed lines are the tournament average.

🎟️ The Race Qualification
Game By Game Group Phase Scores
Under each game, its momentum: every play-by-play action scored with Hollinger's Game Score (a made two is +1.7, a missed shot −0.7, a turnover −1, and so on), each team's recent actions added up with older ones fading out over a minute or so, then one side's total less the other's. The higher the line, the harder that team was pushing at that point in the game.
